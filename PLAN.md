# Faceless "Gift Finds" TikTok - Plan (from Arusha, Tanzania)

## Goal
Earn during Nov-Dec 2026, then reinvest into ads in January.

## Setup (Day 1)
- New TikTok account, handle like @giftfinds-style. Business/creator account.
- Bio: "Gifts under $25 | Link below". Link-in-bio tool (Linktree/Beacons).
- Pick affiliate programs that accept Tanzania + a payout method you can use. VERIFY each:
  Amazon Associates, Digistore24, others. Do not assume approval.
- TikTok Shop affiliate: likely NOT available in Tanzania - check in-app.
- Disclose affiliate links ("link in bio may earn me a commission").

## Daily routine (you have the time - use it)
- Morning: find 6-8 products (reviews, price, commission). Save in the calendar.
- Midday: record screen/product footage (stock clips from the product page, free libraries), edit in CapCut, add voiceover/text.
- Evening: schedule next day's 2 posts. Fill tracker.csv.
- Target: 2 posts/day, 1 extra hour reading comments and studying top videos in the niche.

## Phases
1. Nov 17-23 Test: 6 sub-niches, see which gets watch time.
2. Nov 24-30 Black Friday (Nov 27) / Cyber Monday (Nov 30): deal content, double down on winners.
3. Dec 1-14 Christmas: last-minute and shipping-deadline angle. Stop recommending items that cannot ship in time.

## Rules for deciding
- Keep a series if avg watch >50% or views clearly above your account average. Drop after 5 flops.
- Do not spend on ads until a video earns real clicks/sales organically.

## Caveats
- No income guarantee. Many new accounts earn nothing in the first weeks.
- Verify everything marked VERIFY: programs, payouts, TikTok rules for Tanzania.
- Only recommend products you've checked reviews for. Don't invent claims.
