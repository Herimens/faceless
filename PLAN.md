# Faceless Architectural Drawing Brand (Arusha, Tanzania)

You are a draftsperson. The brand sells: (1) ready-made house plans, (2) custom plans, (3) 3D visuals.
Audience: individuals in Tanzania, diaspora, and villa / safari-camp / lodge clients.
Faceless: videos show drawings and 3D, never you.

## Tools (legal, free)
FreeCAD / LibreCAD (2D), Blender (+BlenderBIM) (3D/render), Sweet Home 3D (quick plans),
SketchUp Free (concepts; check current limits). Do NOT use cracked/pirated software.
(Building your own drawing tool is possible later, but it is months of work - not for this season.)

## LEGAL - verify first (I am not certain of the details)
- I believe building-permit drawings in Tanzania must be prepared/signed by a registered architect (AQRB).
- As a draftsperson: sell concept/sketch plans and 3D visuals clearly labelled "concept - not permit drawings",
  OR partner with a registered architect for permit-ready sets and agree a fee split in writing.
- Check current rules with AQRB / your council. Do not claim approval you don't have.
- Make your own original designs. Don't copy others' plans or sell designs you don't have rights to.

## Products (set YOUR prices - research local rates; I won't guess numbers)
1. Ready-made plan packs: floor plans, elevations, 3D images (PDF). Price in TZS and USD.
   Start with 3 designs: 3-bed family home, 4-bed, small rental block. Add a villa and a camp/lodge concept later.
2. Custom plan: intake form -> concept -> revisions (limit: 2) -> final PDF. Deposit before work.
3. 3D render / walkthrough add-on.
4. Permit-ready set via partner architect (optional, later).
