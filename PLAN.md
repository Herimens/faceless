# Faceless Local-Product TikTok Brand (Arusha, Tanzania)

Model (inferred from a local seller's account): TikTok = shop window. Each video shows a product
and a clear price in Swahili, and the sale closes by WhatsApp/phone. No face needed.
No TikTok Shop, no foreign payout needed. Customers pay in TZS (e.g. M-Pesa / bank).

## Step 1 - Choose ONE niche (Day 1-2)
Pick the niche where you can answer YES to most:
1. Do I have a reliable supplier in Arusha who gives a wholesale price?
2. Can I get product photos/videos from them or shoot them myself?
3. Is profit per sale meaningful (e.g. 10-20% of a 50k-500k TZS sale)?
4. Do buyers ask "bei gani?" - i.e. is it a product people shop for?
5. Can I deliver or hand off delivery (boda, bus parcel, supplier delivery)?
Shortlist to compare (all UNVERIFIED demand - test, don't assume): building/fencing materials, farm supplies
(irrigation, tanks, greenhouse), solar/lighting, furniture, school items for January reopening, event/wedding items.
See supplier-checklist.md.

## Step 2 - Model: broker first, stock later
- Start without inventory: you advertise, the supplier holds stock. Agree commission or margin IN WRITING
  (even a WhatsApp message) before posting prices.
- Never post a price or stock claim you haven't confirmed that day.
- Only buy stock yourself after you've made several sales and know what moves.

## Step 3 - Account setup (Day 1)
- Business-style name = product + town (e.g. "<Product> Arusha"). Bio in Swahili: what you sell, areas, WhatsApp number.
- Use a WhatsApp Business number and a quick-reply template with price list.
- Pinned video: your best price/product video.

## Step 4 - Content (2 posts/day, see content-calendar.csv)
Formats: price video, mistakes to avoid, customer question, finished job, comparison, delivery, year-end.
Every video: hook text in first second, product footage, price, area, "Piga/WhatsApp ..." call to action.
Get a Swahili speaker to check the hook wording in the calendar - I wrote it and may have errors.

## Step 5 - Measure (tracker.csv)
Views are not the goal. Track enquiries, quotes, sales and profit.
Keep a series if it brings enquiries. Drop it after ~5 posts with none.

## Phases
1. Nov 17-23: 10-14 videos to test the niche. Aim: first real enquiries.
2. Nov 24-Dec 7: Double down on the series that brought enquiries.
3. Dec 8-14: Year-end push; reply to every enquiry within the hour.
4. When sales cover costs: put profit into boosting your best video locally (TikTok ads / Promote), targeting Tanzania.

## Risks / honesty
- I can't verify demand, supplier prices or TikTok rules for Tanzania. Verify.
- Don't misrepresent product quality, origin or availability; don't take deposits you can't fulfil.
- Register/handle business licensing and taxes as required (TRA/BRELA) once income is real - check current rules.
- Results are not guaranteed; a seller account with 15K followers may still rely on one viral video.
