# Supplier checklist (ask before you post)
- Product, sizes/options, and wholesale price (TZS)
- Your commission/margin and when/how you get paid
- Stock today? Lead time?
- Delivery: areas, cost, who delivers, who is responsible for damage
- Can they send photos/videos, or can you film at the yard/shop?
- Returns/defects policy
- Written confirmation (WhatsApp) of all of the above
Rule: if you can't confirm it today, don't post it.
