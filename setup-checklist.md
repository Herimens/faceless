# Setup first (TikTok + WhatsApp Business), Blender later

## TikTok account (Day 1)
- Create the account, switch to a Business or Creator account in Settings.
- Name: pick short, searchable, Swahili/English friendly. Ideas (check availability yourself):
  "Ramani za Nyumba Arusha", "Arusha House Plans", "Ramani Hub TZ". Same handle on WhatsApp business name.
- Profile pic: simple logo (text + house icon). Make it free in Canva or similar.
- Bio (edit): "Ramani za nyumba, villa & camps | Ready-made & custom | WhatsApp below / House, villa & camp plans"
- Link: WhatsApp link (wa.me/<your number in international format, no +>) or a link-in-bio page.
- Pin your best video once you have one.
- Check in Settings what features TikTok offers in Tanzania (verify; don't assume Shop/payouts).

## WhatsApp Business (Day 1)
- Install WhatsApp Business (separate from personal). Use a number you'll keep.
- Profile: business name, category, description, hours, location (Arusha), website/TikTok link.
- Catalog: add products (Ready-made plan, Custom plan, 3D render) with your price once decided.
- Quick replies: paste templates from whatsapp-templates.md (shortcuts like /bei, /karibu).
- Greeting + away messages on. Labels: New / Quote sent / Deposit paid / Delivered.

## Week 1 content with NO Blender (phone only)
Use original work only. Film paper, not other people's designs.
1. Hand-sketch floor plan time-lapse (phone on a stand, overhead view).
2. "Makosa 3 ya ramani ya nyumba" - text-on-screen tips over sketches.
3. Poll/question: "Chumba ngapi kwa nyumba ya familia?"
4. Explain: plan vs permit drawing (honest, builds trust).
5. Plot-to-plan: show how you start from a plot sketch.
6. Intro video: who you are as a service, what you offer, how to order (no face).
7. FAQ: "Ramani inachukua muda gani?"
Post 1-2/day; reply to every comment within the hour.

## Before posting prices
Finalize your price list and deposit terms. Don't post prices you haven't decided.

## Done when
- TikTok bio + link live; WhatsApp Business catalog + quick replies set; 5 videos posted.
